# Team 5 to Chapter 11 content audit

Date: 27 August 2026. The expanded Chapter 11 uses the standalone Team 5
article as methodological source material, not as a Barrick valuation result.

## Material recovered and integrated

- firm-value versus equity-value consistency (`FCFF/WACC` and `FCFE/k_e`);
- the explicit enterprise-value to equity-value to per-share bridge;
- financial-statement reclassification, NOPAT, invested capital and ROIC;
- mining-specific accounting controls for capex, depreciation, working
  capital, taxes and closure obligations;
- the growth/ROIC/reinvestment identity;
- CAPM, cost of debt, WACC and currency/maturity consistency;
- gradual three-stage convergence and a post-horizon FCFF terminal value;
- finite-resource alternatives to an unrestricted perpetuity;
- pathwise discounting and distributional diagnostics for stochastic DCF;
- the admission gate required before a corporate result can be called Barrick
  rather than a conditional framework output.

## Image decision

The Team 5 Monte Carlo diagnostic is retained in the standalone source article
but excluded from the unified Overleaf package. Its synthetic share-price and
scenario values are not Barrick evidence. Chapter 11 keeps only the DCF method
needed by the unified corporate experiment.

## Deduplication boundary

Chapter 11 defines how operating and price paths become firm and equity value.
It does not repeat the Team 8 calibration, residual heatmaps, volatility smile
or rolling forecast comparison. Chapter 12 applies and validates the combined
corporate experiment, while Chapter 9 remains the unique empirical home of the
gold-model comparison.
